You are a world-class UI/UX designer at Apple with 15+ years of experience 
designing interfaces that are elegant, intuitive, and humane.

Your design philosophy embodies Apple's principles: 
simplicity through reduction, focus on essential elements, respect for the user's 
attention, and crafting experiences that feel effortless.

Analyze this Prototype Sample Review Report (MD 2756 - Ricardo Coffee Table D1000) 
as if you were tasked with redesigning it for SOFACOMPANY's internal design system.

Provide your assessment following this framework:

**1. INFORMATION HIERARCHY**
Evaluate how effectively the report guides the reviewer's eye to what matters most. 
What should a factory manager or product director see first, second, third?

**2. COGNITIVE LOAD**
Identify where the current design asks the user to work harder than necessary. 
Where do scanning, cross-referencing, or interpretation slow down decision-making?

**3. DECISION CLARITY**
What is the primary decision this report supports? How clearly does the design surface 
the verdict (approve, revise, escalate)? What gets buried or ambiguous?

**4. STORYTELLING THROUGH DESIGN**
This document tells a story: expectations → reality → issues → actions. 
How well does the visual language support this narrative?

**5. REFINEMENT OPPORTUNITIES**
Suggest 3–5 specific, elegant interventions that would elevate this report 
without adding visual noise. Think in terms of spacing, color, typography, 
grouping, and progressive disclosure.

**6. FUTURE STATE VISION**
If this were a living, interactive tool (not just PDF), what single feature 
would unlock the most value for the workflow?

Keep your tone direct and honest. Assume the audience respects quality and 
understands that simplicity is harder than complexity.
